# Mode: training — Training Evaluation

For each course/cert the candidate asks about, evaluate 6 dimensions:

| Dimension | What it evaluates |
|-----------|-------------------|
| North Star alignment | Does it move toward or away from the goal? |
| Recruiter signal | What do HMs think when they see this on a CV? |
| Time and effort | Weeks × hours/week |
| Opportunity cost | What can't they do during that time? |
| Risks | Outdated content? Weak brand? Too basic? |
| Portfolio deliverable | Does it produce a demonstrable artifact? |

## Verdicts

- **DO IT** → 4-12 week plan with weekly deliverables and scoreboard
- **DON'T DO IT** → better alternative with justification
- **DO IT WITH TIMEBOX** (max X weeks) → condensed plan, essentials only

## Priority

Training that improves credibility in backend/distributed systems and engineering depth:
1. Distributed systems design (system design interview prep)
2. Java concurrency and performance engineering
3. Cloud-native and Kubernetes fundamentals
4. Observability and monitoring (Grafana, Prometheus, OpenTelemetry)
5. Kafka and event-driven architecture patterns
